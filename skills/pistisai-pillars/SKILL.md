# Pistisai Pillars

Pistisai is built on four pillars. Each pillar corresponds to a Titan, a cardinal direction, and a role.

## Source

Sourced from `assets/brand/brand-framework.md` in the pistisai-app repo (French original, verified 2026-11-15). The English below is a translation of that source; the Titan/direction/role mapping and palette match the source exactly.

## The four pillars

| Pillar | Titan | Direction | Domain | Role |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Aiman (The Face) | Hyperion | East (Dawn) | "He who watches," god of celestial light | The face and the welcome |
| Aigent (The Engine) | Koios / Coeus | North (Axis) | "Questioning," god of intellect | Intelligence and structure |
| Aidration (The Flow) | Krios / Crius | South (Ram) | "The Ram," god of the constellations | Orchestration and rhythm |
| Aimotions (The Heart) | Iapetos | West (Sunset) | "The Piercer," father of Prometheus | Emotion and inner fire |

## The myth

The four Titans were the cosmic pillars holding sky and earth apart, one at each cardinal point. Without them there is no space for life. Pistisai (Πίστις) is the daemon of trust, honesty, and good faith, one of the few good spirits to escape Pandora's box, and she fled to Olympus, leaving humanity behind. The name means: bring trust (Pistisai) back among humans, carried by four pillars.

## Identity notes

- The logo architecture is a stoa (portico): a pediment with the name, four columns of distinct textures, and a base.
- The Stoa Poikile (Painted Stoa) was a real building on the Athenian agora where Zeno taught Stoicism. The concept is a "portico of trust" for the AI era.

Background `#181A20`, card `#1E2230`, gold primary `#FFD700`, gold secondary `#FFE44D`, gold accent `#D4A017`, gold shadow `#B8860B`, primary text `#F5E6C8`, secondary text `#B8A88A`, border `#2A2A3E`.
